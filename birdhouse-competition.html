Birdhouse Competition

A competition entry for a sculptural birdhouse set in a circular pond. The spherical form uses mahogany cladding, teak flooring and tempered glass, with openings and indigenous planting designed for local birdlife.

The full set covers elevations, floor plans, roof plan, section details and renderings — from structural steel and bolted connections through to drainage and habitat finishes. Awarded 2nd place.
