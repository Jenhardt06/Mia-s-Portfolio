Environmental Centre

A two-wing environmental centre organised around a planted courtyard, designed for visitors exploring the surrounding landscape. One wing holds the mountain café, outdoor equipment shop, reception and exhibition spaces; the other contains administration, first aid and service rooms.

Large glazed façades, gabled roofs and a viewing terrace open the building to the mountains, while the floor and roof plans set out the layout, solar array and construction detailing.
