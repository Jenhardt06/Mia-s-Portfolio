Sport Centre

A community sports centre organised around a full-size indoor hall, with reception, administration, changing rooms, first aid and equipment storage arranged along the front of the building.

Brick elevations, a covered entrance and rooftop solar panels give the building its character, while the section and structural framing drawings set out the steel roof, wall construction and hall volume.
