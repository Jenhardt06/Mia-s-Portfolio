ABOUT THE DESIGNER

Turning technical drawings into real spaces.

Mia Hills
Computer-Aided Draughting & Design

I am a dedicated and detail-oriented Computer-Aided Draughting and Design student with a strong work ethic and a passion for developing high-quality technical and design solutions. I enjoy transforming ideas into detailed digital drawings and developing practical design concepts with a focus on accuracy, functionality and visual presentation. Currently developing my skills in AutoCAD, Autodesk Revit and Autodesk Inventor, I have a growing interest in architectural design, technical drawing and 3D modelling. I approach every project with attention to detail, a willingness to learn and a commitment to producing work to a high standard.

Alongside my technical studies, I have developed strong teamwork, communication and leadership skills through sport and student involvement. My experience as a first-team hockey player, participation in an international team tour to the Netherlands, and involvement in the Student Representative Council have taught me the importance of responsibility, collaboration and working effectively as part of a team. I am excited to continue developing my technical and creative abilities while gaining practical industry experience and contributing to meaningful design projects.
