Community Centre

A community learning, digital skills and resource centre organised around a double-height reception and lounge. The ground floor holds a computer laboratory, training classroom, library and public waiting area, with a loft above for a small meeting room, staff office and tea point.

The building is defined by a glazed barrel-vault roof, shown in night-time renderings of the entrance. Site, floor, roof and section drawings set out the layout, parking and construction beside the Tugela River.
